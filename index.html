The Brief

The industry
builds them to
break.

Recreational Time takes a hard look at what really happens behind modern RV manufacturing, where corners get cut, materials get lower quality, and expensive repairs become "normal ownership." These guides help you understand the problems before they drain your wallet or ruin your time on the road.

From travel trailers and motorhomes to fifth wheels and camper vans, these guides uncover the maintenance mistakes, hidden damage, and overlooked routines that cost RV owners thousands over time, while also simplifying everyday RV life with practical meal planning, campsite cooking, and organization systems.

Owning an RV is one of the greatest freedoms in modern life. But unlike a standard vehicle or a stationary home, an RV is both a moving mechanical system and a living space, each with its own demands. Most first-time owners have no idea what they've taken on until something goes wrong, and by then the repair bill can run into the thousands.

These guides exist to close that gap, the hard-won wisdom of experienced RVers, mechanics, and technicians, distilled into three field-edited volumes that tell you exactly what to do, when to do it, and what the industry would rather you didn't notice.

Common
Questions.

About the guides and practical RV ownership.

Why do modern RVs fail so much sooner than older rigs?
Modern RVs are often built faster and lighter than older rigs, with more complex systems, thinner materials, and tighter manufacturing margins. Features that improve showroom appeal can also introduce more stress points, maintenance demands, and long-term failure risks if they aren't properly maintained.
Are the guides specific to one class of RV?
No. The guides are written for the most common RV types, including motorhomes, fifth wheels, camper vans, and travel trailers. The maintenance principles stay largely the same, while specific systems and layouts are explained where needed.
I'm a first-time RV owner. Which volume do I need most?
Start with Volume I — The Maintenance Guide. The 20-minute pre-trip inspection alone prevents the most common new-owner disasters: blown tires from neglected pressure, leaks from missed sealant checks, burst pipes from improper winterizing. If you're still shopping, the Complete Field Library adds meal planning, campsite cooking, and bonus reference material.
What's included in the Complete Field Library?
The Complete Field Library combines the RV Owners Maintenance Guide and the Recipe & Meal Guide. Together, they cover preventive maintenance, seasonal service, pre-trip inspections, campground cooking, pantry systems, meal planning, and everyday RV routines that make life on the road simpler and less expensive.
How much can the Maintenance Guide actually save me?
Preventative maintenance is almost always cheaper than repair work. Catching a roof leak can prevent $8,000–$15,000 in structural damage, while routine inspections and seasonal maintenance often save owners hundreds or even thousands per year in dealership service costs.
Is the Recipe Guide useful if I only RV on weekends?
Yes, the Recipe & Meal Planning Guide is designed for owners who don't want to think about food planning every Friday. One stock-up, one shopping list, every weekend trip handled. The campfire and no-fridge chapters add range for dry-camping or off-grid weekends where galley power isn't reliable.
What format are the guides delivered in?
Each guide is delivered as a downloadable PDF immediately after purchase. You can read them on any device, including phones, tablets, computers, or print the seasonal checklists and pantry lists to keep in the rig.
