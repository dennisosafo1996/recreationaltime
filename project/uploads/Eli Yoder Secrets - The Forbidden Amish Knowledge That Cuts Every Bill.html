Everything you need to know before you order

Do these methods actually work in my climate?
Most methods work universally. A few (like the earth tunnel) are climate-dependent — they perform exceptionally in hot-dry and temperate climates, and need adaptation in hot-humid or high-water-table areas. Volume III includes specific adaptations for 5 climate zones (hot-dry, hot-humid, temperate, cold, and apartment) so you know exactly which methods apply to your situation and which to skip.
How long until I actually see savings on my bills?
The product swaps in Week 2 (soap recipe, DE pest control, water heater adjustment) show up on your next billing cycle — typically 28-35 days. The bigger savings from the radiant barrier and earth tunnel appear within 60 days. By the end of the 90-day program, most readers see 30-50% reduction in cooling-related bills and complete elimination of recurring pest control and cleaning product costs.
What if I'm a renter or live in an apartment?
Volume II works fully for renters — soap recipes, fermentation, cleaning chemistry, food storage. Volume I has selective applications (water filtration, DE indoors, electrical efficiency). The earth tunnel and Kachelofen sections won't apply, but case study #5 in Volume III documents a Cambridge renter who still cut his controllable bills by 43%. About 30% of the program applies to renters at very low project cost.
I'm in my 60s or 70s. Can I still do this?
Yes. Volume III includes a dedicated Senior-Friendly Modifications section that addresses the program by physical limitation: "can't dig", "can't climb a ladder", "can't lift heavy things", "working alone". Case studies #1 (66-year-old in Tennessee) and #4 (74/76-year-old couple in Vermont) show the program working with full hire-out at higher project cost but the same long-term savings.
