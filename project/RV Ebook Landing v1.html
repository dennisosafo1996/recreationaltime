Before You
Order.

If your question isn't here, send it to the senior-engineer Q&A queue — we reply within one business day.

Which volume should I start with?
If you don't yet own an RV, start with Volume I — The Buyer's Dossier. If you do, start with Volume II — The Maintenance Log. If you're shopping and travelling in the same year, the Complete Field Edition sequences both for you and adds Volume III, the destinations atlas.
Does this cover travel trailers and fifth wheels, not just motorhomes?
Yes — every volume covers the five common form factors: Class A, Class B, Class C, fifth wheel, and travel trailer. Where work diverges (e.g. chassis service on Class A vs. hitch maintenance on a fifth wheel), the volumes split into clearly-marked tracks.
What format are the volumes delivered in?
Each volume is delivered as a downloadable PDF, plus an ePub for tablets. The maintenance log also ships as a printable spiral-bound workbook for the service bay. Bundle buyers receive all three formats at no additional charge.
How current is the 2026 edition?
The library was last revised in March 2026. Pricing, registration rules, and the dealer-markup tables are accurate as of that revision. Bundle buyers receive every revision through the 2027 edition at no additional charge.
Is there a refund policy?
Yes — seven days, no questions. If the work isn't useful, write to the support address and we refund in full. The volumes remain yours.
I'm not a mechanic. Can I actually do the work in Volume II?
About 70% of the protocol is owner-level — fluid checks, roof seams, slide-out cleaning, electrical inspection. The remaining 30% is explicitly marked "take it in", with the dealer-markup data so you can negotiate the invoice when you do.
